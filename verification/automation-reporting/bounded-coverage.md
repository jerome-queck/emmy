The [accounting revision](bounded-accounting.md) separates native evidence from process diagnostics. The accounting source and fixture must remain unchanged during an exact acceptance flight. Reports bind source hashes, source revision, shape digest and each cumulative ticket. Preliminary development results with changed loaded bytes cannot establish exact-head acceptance. Public neutral names preserve individual source counts and byte totals. Actual namespace names and exact configuration remain private.

Assumptions include cooperating immutable producer files, ordinary private directories, exact complete raw materialization, collision-resistant hashes and trusted caller attribution of actual adapter responses. The final seal is a bounded sequence of observations, not an atomic filesystem snapshot. No remote effect, provider authentication, user delivery, schedule firing, globally latest history or operational admission follows from these tests. Native file-reference registration and any cloud consumer budget remain separate owner acceptance.

The budget and final metadata-fence guards have paired actual-source baseline, mutant and restored controls in `test/automation-reporting-bounded-mutations.test.mjs`. Named contradiction faults and cross-process owner-turn races are behavioral controls, not an exhaustive staged-sequence or power-loss proof. Hard-link exclusivity and immutable predecessor bytes define the local commit ordering. A delayed already committed grant return remains historical; no atomic remote execution or cancellation is claimed. These controls do not exhaust every staged sequence, concurrent suspension or power-loss cutoff. Durable operating-system persistence beyond the exercised hard-link/file synchronization assumptions remains unproved. Independent exact-head review, minimal-controller authority and scoped accounting acceptance remain separate. The existing v1 exact96 cutoff oracle and eight source pairs keep their original requirements. Passing v1 cannot fill these new gaps. The aggregate gate must retain stale-pin refusal until independent review accepts the new source closure.
